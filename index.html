How to use this
  1. Follow the three setup steps on first use: your cards, balances and programs. Change them any time under Home → Edit, or Settings.
  2. Home shows what your points are worth, the top 5 ways to redeem them in ₹ per point, a plan for your balances, and the value of points you already hold.
  3. Trips shows what a route costs in your card points, and whether your balance and yearly cap allow it. Check a booking turns a cash price and a points price into ₹ per point.
  4. Rates and mile values are published estimates. Edit any of them and the rankings update.
  5. Deals is a snapshot and does not update by itself. Use Settings → Update to refresh it, and confirm every offer on the bank or program site before you transfer.

    Found an award flight or hotel night? Enter what it would cost in cash and in points to see its real ₹ per point, and whether to pay cash or use points. You can pay with card points or with airline and hotel points you already hold.
